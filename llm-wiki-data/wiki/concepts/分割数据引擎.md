# 分割数据引擎

分割数据引擎是 [[2304.02643v1 (1)|Segment Anything]] 中模型改进与数据收集相互促进的三阶段流程。第一阶段由标注员在 [[SAM]] 辅助下交互绘制 masks；第二阶段由模型预填充高置信度 masks，标注员补充遗漏对象；第三阶段则完全自动生成 [[SA-1B]]。（PDF pp. 5–7，§4）

> **英文对应表述（非逐字原文）：** The data engine moves from model-assisted manual annotation to semi-automatic completion and finally to fully automatic mask generation as the model improves.

辅助人工阶段产生约 120k 图像和 4.3M masks，半自动阶段新增约 180k 图像和 5.9M masks，最终自动阶段覆盖 11M 图像并生成约 1.1B masks。数据引擎各阶段均改善模型表现，仅使用自动数据相对全部数据约低 `0.5 mIoU`。（PDF pp. 5–7、10–11，§4 与§5.5）

> **英文对应表述（非逐字原文）：** The three stages scale from millions of assisted masks to roughly 1.1 billion automatic masks, and ablations indicate that each stage contributes to model quality.

自动阶段依赖多尺度点网格、NMS、predicted-IoU、稳定性筛选和连通分量后处理。公开数据的标签来源因此是模型推断，而非经人工逐一确认的 ground truth。（PDF p. 18，§B；PDF pp. 27–29，Dataset Card）

> **英文对应表述（非逐字原文）：** The released masks are model-generated outputs filtered by geometric and confidence heuristics rather than individually verified manual annotations.

早期人工阶段由 130 名位于 Kenya 的标注员完成。对象粒度、成组对象是否拆分及 stuff/thing 判断具有主观性；论文描述了培训、QA 抽检和每周反馈，但没有给出跨群体 disagreement 研究。（PDF pp. 28–29，Data Annotation Card）

> **英文对应表述（非逐字原文）：** Human annotation involved 130 workers in Kenya and included training and quality review, but object granularity remained subjective and no systematic cross-group disagreement analysis was reported.
